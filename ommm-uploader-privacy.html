Privacy Policy

OMMM Uploader · last updated 12 November 2026

Summary

OMMM Uploader is a private tool used only by its owner to upload and schedule his own videos on his own YouTube channels. It does not collect, sell or share anyone's personal data.

Data the app accesses

  • YouTube account access. When the owner signs in with Google, the app receives permission to upload and manage videos and playlists on the channel he selects, and to read that channel's analytics.
  • Channel information. Channel name, video list, and view and watch-time figures for the owner's own channels.

How the data is used

Only to upload, schedule and organise the owner's videos, and to show him how those videos perform. Google user data is not used for advertising, is not used to train AI models, and is not transferred to anyone else.

Where the data is stored

The Google sign-in token is stored in a file on the owner's own computer. Nothing is stored on a server run by this app. Videos and their details go to YouTube through Google's official API.

Sharing

No data is shared with third parties.

Removing access

Access can be removed at any time at myaccount.google.com/permissions. Deleting the token file on the computer also ends the app's access.

Google API Services

The app's use of information received from Google APIs follows the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements. The app uses YouTube API Services; see the YouTube Terms of Service and the Google Privacy Policy.
