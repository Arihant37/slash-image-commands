OMMM Uploader

OMMM Uploader is a private desktop tool. It uploads and schedules videos on the YouTube channels that belong to its owner, and reads those channels' own statistics so the owner can see how his videos perform.

What it does

  • Uploads the owner's own videos to his own YouTube channels and sets the title, description, tags, thumbnail and publish time.
  • Adds those videos to the owner's playlists.
  • Reads view and watch-time figures for those channels.

Who can use it

Only the owner, signed in with his own Google account. It is not offered to the public and has no sign-up.

How it uses Google data

It asks for YouTube permissions (upload, manage, and read analytics) and uses them only for the actions listed above. Details are in the privacy policy.
